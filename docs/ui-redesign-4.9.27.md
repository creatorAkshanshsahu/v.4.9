# AK TV cinematic interface — 4.9.27

This refresh introduces an original AK monogram, a skippable animated brand entrance, and an immersive poster-led home experience. It takes broad cues from premium streaming interfaces without reproducing another service's logo, artwork, sounds, or exact layout.

## Home and section behavior

- The selected title's TMDB artwork now works as a wide, edge-to-edge home backdrop behind the first shelf. Channel selection remains a native, muted Media3 preview; its sampled channel palette lights the hero and progress accents.
- Live, Always on, Movies, and Series receive distinct section accents and context labels. Focus on a channel, movie, or series updates the spotlight, actions, metadata, and relevant programme or title art. Moving down the shelves gracefully folds the hero away; returning to the lead shelf brings it back.
- Poster shelves use a clean, image-first treatment with visible focus and responsive TV/phone sizing. The catalogue and detail pages remain native; TMDB identity, exact episode selection, and native source controls are unchanged.

## Entrance and identity

- The launcher, navigation rail, detail pages, and players share a redesigned geometric AK mark.
- The entrance is a short animated logo reveal with an ambient ring and light sweep. It can still be skipped immediately, respects the saved sound toggle, and uses only the app's original sound mark.

## Players

- Live TV keeps native Media3 and receives a warmer live lower-third, clearly focused guide/source/quality/retry actions, and the existing channel guide. No web player or alternate playback path is introduced.
- Movie and series playback keep native Media3, audio/subtitle selection, source controls, resume behavior, and exact TMDB episode identity. The redesigned control dock sits over a cinematic lower scrim and uses distinct movie/series accents.
- Layout and color changes do not change network requests, stream selection, codecs, DRM, credentials, or playback policy.
